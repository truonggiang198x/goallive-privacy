Privacy Policy — GoalLive

Effective date: 2 November 2026 · App: GoalLive (package com.studio.goallive)

GoalLive is a free live football scores app. We do not require an account, a login, or any registration to use the app, and the developer does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • GoalLive has no user accounts — there is nothing to sign up for, and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your match reminders and language choice are stored only on your device and never sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

GoalLive shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may collect certain device information to serve and measure ads, which can include your device advertising ID, IP address, and approximate location. This data is handled under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Sports data

To show fixtures, live scores, standings, and top scorers, the app requests publicly available match data from third-party sports-data providers. These requests contain no personal information about you — only the data needed to answer the request (e.g. which league or match to load).

4. Device permissions

GoalLive requests only the permissions it needs to work:

  • Internet / network state — to load live scores and schedules.
  • Notifications — only if you tap the bell on a match, to remind you 15 minutes before kick-off. All reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Exact alarms / run at startup — to keep your match reminders working reliably after a reboot.

5. Children

GoalLive is a general-audience sports app and is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
